# CONTENT MAP

## Scenario layer
- `common/defines/JRK_defines.lua` — 2005-01-01 start, 2012 end.
- `common/bookmarks/JRK_2005.txt` — dedicated East/West Japan bookmark.
- `common/country_tags/00_JRK_countries.txt` — EJP/WJP tags.
- `common/countries/` — country colors/cultures.
- `history/countries/` — politics, technologies, characters, starting ideas and OOB references.
- `history/states/` — newly authored overrides on vanilla Japan state IDs; no custom map geometry.

## Narrative/gameplay layer
- `common/national_focus/JRK_EJP_focus.txt` — East story/industry/military branches.
- `common/national_focus/JRK_WJP_focus.txt` — West crisis/defence/industry/military branches.
- `events/JRK_events.txt` — scenario opening and outbreak events.
- `common/on_actions/JRK_on_actions.txt` — one-time opening events only; no daily polling.
- `common/decisions/` — limited operational decisions for short-term actions.

## Military layer
- `history/units/EJP_2005.txt`
- `history/units/WJP_2005.txt`

These contain starting division templates, Army HQ templates, Regimental Support rows, initial divisions, tank/air designer variants, stockpiles and production lines.

## State/system layer
- `common/ideas/JRK_ideas.txt` — persistent national conditions.
- `common/dynamic_modifiers/JRK_dynamic_modifiers.txt` — staged/timed operational conditions.

## Localisation
- `localisation/japanese/JRK_l_japanese.yml` — primary authored localisation.

## Story spine
1. East prepares the operation.
2. Attack on the fusion reactor in Toki City.
3. Takeover of the high-firepower laser base linked to the reactor.
4. Hacking/disruption of West Japan's air-defence network.
5. 日本アルプス大突破 and open war.
6. Tokai becomes the first major operational objective on the coarse vanilla-state map.

## Intentionally absent
- `map/`
- custom province definitions
- custom strategic regions
- old-mod state geometry
- invented formal names for the reactor/laser facility/operation
- discarded AI-origin lore
